# Changelog

This page lists what changed on [elegantfrontend.training](https://elegantfrontend.training), newest first. It is written for learners: it says what you can now do, and it is not a list of commits. Links are paths on the site.

The record starts on 2026-09-20, the day the practice site went live. The seven templates (`npx elegant`) and the 45 Agent Skills came before that; you will find them in the [README](https://github.com/grvpanchal/elegant#readme) and [`skills/`](https://github.com/grvpanchal/elegant/tree/master/skills).

## 2026-10-17

### Content
- **Four free courses with 28 lessons, and no signup.** They are [UI layer](/courses/ui-layer/), [State layer](/courses/state-layer/), [Server layer](/courses/server-layer/) and [Harnessing AI with Agent Skills](/courses/agent-skills/).
  - Each lesson teaches one term and links the Agent Skill that encodes it, plus a practice question to try next.
  - Each lesson shows its reading time and links to the lessons before and after it.
  - When you mark a lesson complete, the course page shows your progress.
  - Start at [Courses](/courses/).

### Product
- **[Dashboard](/dashboard/).** It shows:
  - the questions you have solved, by format, difficulty and layer;
  - your streak and your last twelve weeks of activity;
  - your progress through courses, study plans and terms;
  - the question to do next.

  Your browser works all of this out from the progress you have saved.
- **[Pricing](/pricing/).** Free is the whole site, and it stays that way. Pro and Lifetime are there to fund more courses and questions. Their checkout runs in PayPal's sandbox, so no real money moves, and their prices are not set yet.
- **Where your progress is kept.** The dashboard and the [account page](/account/) now tell you where your progress is: synced to your account, or on this device only.

### Community
- **The repository now has a public [roadmap](https://github.com/grvpanchal/elegant/blob/master/ROADMAP.md) and a [contribution guide](https://github.com/grvpanchal/elegant/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).** It also has [issue forms](https://github.com/grvpanchal/elegant/issues/new/choose) for three things: reporting a bug, correcting a lesson, and asking for a skill or a question.

## 2026-10-11

### Content
- ***Elegant Frontend*, the whole glossary as one free PDF book.** It has three parts (UI, Server and State) and one chapter per term. Each chapter gives:
  - the term's summary and key insight;
  - a code example;
  - the common mistakes;
  - the key principles and quality gates of the term's Agent Skill.

  Download it from the [book page](/book/) or from the home page.

### Product
- **A redesigned home page** that uses the same visual language as the rest of the site.
- **Sharper icons** for every term.

## 2026-09-20

### Content
- **The [practice bank](/practice/): 47 questions in five formats.** There are 12 quiz, 13 coding, 10 UI-coding, 6 system-design and 6 harness questions. Each coding question runs its tests in a workspace in your browser. Press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) to run them.
- **[Study plans](/plans/).** Each plan's time budget adds up to the questions it contains, and finishing a plan earns a printable certificate.
- **Three [curated lists](/lists/)** that tell you where to start.
- **Interview-loop guides** for [Google](/guides/google-frontend-loop.html), [Meta](/guides/meta-frontend-loop.html), [Amazon](/guides/amazon-frontend-loop.html) and [Stripe](/guides/stripe-frontend-loop.html). Each guide maps the company's rounds to questions in the bank.
- **The [blog](/blog/): 101 posts on frontend architecture in the AI era.** Each post has a code example, a diagram and a practice question.

### Product
- **An [account page](/account/).** You can keep your progress under a named profile in this browser, and export it as a file to move it to another one. You never need an account to learn.
- **A menu that works on phones.**
- **A home page that leads with the harness.**

    Questions

    Is anything locked behind a paid tier?
    No. Free is the whole site: courses, questions, plans, skills and the dashboard. Paying funds more of it.
    Is this real money?
    Not while the page says Sandbox. PayPal's sandbox uses test buyer accounts; nothing is charged.
    Who checks that a payment happened?
    The CFO office verifies orders server-side against PayPal. This page only records the order ID PayPal returned.
    What do the paid tiers cost?
    Prices are being set by the CFO office. Until then the tiers show "Price to be set" and the sandbox uses a test amount.

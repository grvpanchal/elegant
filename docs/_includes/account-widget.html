{%- comment -%} Account state for the site nav: who is signed in, and what kind of identity it is. "Account" is an email sign-in verified by the identity provider; "Device profile" is a name typed into this browser and verified by nothing — the menu says which, so the two are never mistaken for each other. Signed in, the nav shows an initial and the (truncated) name; the menu holds the full name, its kind, the account link and Sign out, so eleven links and an email address fit one row. Backed by docs/assets/js/account.js (painting, the menu) and account-supabase.js (the verified session, restored on every page). {%- endcomment -%}

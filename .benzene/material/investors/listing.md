# Investor listing: elegantfrontend.training

Draft. Nothing here is sent or posted; the CEO decides. Every number carries its record and the date it was read.

## One line

Free frontend training for developers displaced by AI: every lesson is a UI, Server or State term paired with the
Agent Skill that lets a coding agent's frontend output be briefed, checked and reviewed.

## Stage

Pre-revenue, before the audience floor. The free site answers at https://elegantfrontend.training/ (home, practice bank,
glossary); the courses, dashboard and pricing pages are built in the repository but returned 404 on the live domain on
2026-10-17. Paid tiers are CFO proposals awaiting the CEO, and checkout runs only in PayPal sandbox
(`.benzene/material/finance/pricing.md`, 2026-10-17). The organisation is AI-run: 17 skill cells across the CTO, CMO,
CXO, CFO and CIO offices plus the COO planner (`.benzene/benzene-org.yaml`, `.benzene/benzene.yaml`), with one human
CEO who approves every item before anything goes out.

If you have this weekend and want to start, start here.

The One week plan is seven sessions of roughly an hour across UI, State and Server, 345 minutes in all. In each one you read the concept pages, then do the practice questions that follow them, with real tests: what an atom is and where its boundary goes, where props stop and containers start, how a store, a reducer and a selector split the work.

It's written for people who already write JavaScript for a living. You won't learn syntax. You'll learn the words you brief an agent in, and the checks you hold its work to.

Next to it is the book: Elegant Frontend, 354 pages, one chapter per term, UI, Server and State. Each chapter puts the term's summary, its code, its common mistakes and its Agent Skill's quality gates side by side. It's a free PDF, with no email asked.

Both are free. Neither needs an account. Your progress stays in your browser.

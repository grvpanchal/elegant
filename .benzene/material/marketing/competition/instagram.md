# Instagram: the report records no competitor post

The CMO report (fetched 2026-10-17) **could not see any competitor's Instagram posts**. Profiles sit behind a login
wall, help.instagram.com renders as an empty JavaScript shell, and only the follower and post counts in the
`og:description` served to `facebookexternalhit/1.1` were readable. In its words: "We could not see any competitor's
mix of Reels, carousels and single images." No best post is recorded, and every number field above is empty on
purpose.

**What it does record: audience only.**

| Master.dev (@frontendmasters) | 46K | 589 | [E47] |
| GreatFrontEnd | 43K | 615 | [E25] |
| Frontend Mentor | 721 | 63 | [E66] |
| **@elegantfrontend** | **9** | **0** | [E7] |

**The bar we hold instead.**

- Carousels of 2 to 10 slides, JPEG, 4:5 at 1080×1350 [E209][E210]; captions of at most 2,200 characters [E209].
- At most 5 hashtags. Meta's API still allows 30; 5 is our own conservative choice, because the reported 5-hashtag cap
  is Unverified in the report.
- Reels "make up over 50% of all time spent on Instagram" [E156], and Reels over 3 minutes "won't be recommended to new
  audiences" [E157]. The twelve-week plan adds one Reel a week next to two carousels.

**The bar for us.**

- Volume beats size. The channels that grew uploaded hundreds of videos. roadmap.sh shows what stopping costs: 220K
  subscribers and no upload in over a year. The bar is one Short a week, every week.
- A Short is vertical or square and up to 3 minutes [E218]. We keep ours to 30–60 seconds: one term, one failure, one fix.
- Title at most 100 characters, description at most 5,000 bytes, tags at most 500 characters, none with `<` or `>`
  [E217]. The Data API rejects anything over.
- For the first twelve weeks the bar is the cadence, not the views: our channel starts at zero.

# Held for later: terminology lessons

The CEO's call on 2026-10-20: "Remove education posts on terminology that comes later when I have enough followers.
Target audience necessities."

Posts that teach one frontend term (selectors, skeletons, state pairs) wait here until the CEO says the audience is
big enough. Until then every post speaks to what the audience needs: AI slop that gets past review, the work that is
left for a developer once an agent writes the code, and the harness that prevents slop.

Nothing here is a channel's material: the rooms, the outbox and the material checks read `posts/`, `slides/` and
`video/` only. Each post's `slides:` already points at `later/slides/`. To bring a campaign back, move its files to
`posts/`, `slides/` and `video/`, change `later/slides/` back to `slides/` in each `slides:` line, and re-date the posts.

# Business Model

## Who Pays
SIMULATED data indicates that developers aged 25-40, particularly those recently laid off or transitioning roles, are the primary user base. The target demographic assumes a 1% conversion rate from free to paid users, based on the competition's observed conversion (Scrimba 2022 revenue NOK 18.7M at an operating result of NOK -0.3M; Frontend Mentor $1.6k MRR from 284 subscribers two weeks after PRO).

## For What
Users pay for structured interview preparation, algorithmic problem-solving libraries, and community mentorship. The core value proposition is reducing time-to-hire during unemployment gaps. The between-jobs discount (40% off annual and lifetime) is self-declared, targeting developers without a salary.

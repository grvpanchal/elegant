# Pricing for Developers Between Jobs

## Free Tier
Designed for those exploring options while unemployed. Includes the full glossary of UI, Server and State terms, 47 practice questions with an in-browser workspace, and 4 free courses with 28 lessons. Price: $0.

## Monthly Tier
For developers actively job hunting who need focused practice. Provides the full problem library, detailed walkthroughs, and priority support. Price: $15/month.

## Annual Tier
Best value for extended job searches. Includes offline access and exclusive webinars. Price: $120/year. With the between-jobs discount (40% off), it drops to $72.

## Lifetime Tier
A one-time investment for long-term career resilience. Includes early feature access and a founder badge. Price: $180. With the between-jobs discount (40% off), it drops to $108.

All payable prices remain above PayPal's $10 fee floor. A 30-day unconditional refund policy applies to all paid tiers.
